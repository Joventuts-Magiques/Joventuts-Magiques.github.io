## General Description

The Mind is a cooperative game designed by Wolfgang Warsch and published by NSV in 2018. In this unique game, all players work together as one team: they must play their numbered cards in ascending order without any verbal or gestural communication. You can only rely on your mental connection with your teammates to know when it's the right moment to play your card. Simple to explain, yet extraordinarily difficult to master!

## Game Components

- **100 numbered cards** (from 1 to 100)
- **12 level cards** (from 1 to 12)
- **5 life cards**
- **3 Shuriken cards** (throwing stars)

## Game Objective

Complete all the levels in the game by playing cards in ascending order without making mistakes. Each level adds one more card per player, making it progressively more challenging!

## Setup

### Number of Levels per Game

| Players | Levels | Starting Lives | Starting Shurikens |
|---------|--------|----------------|--------------------|
| 2       | 12     | 2              | 1                  |
| 3       | 10     | 3              | 1                  |
| 4       | 8      | 4              | 1                  |

### Initial Configuration

1. Stack the appropriate **level cards** face-up in ascending order (with level 1 on top). Unused level cards go back in the box
2. Place the starting **lives** and **shurikens** face-up on the table. Keep the rest to one side — they'll be used later if needed
3. Shuffle the **100 numbered cards** and deal each player as many cards as the current level (one card each for level 1). Place remaining cards face-down to one side
4. Each player **looks at their cards in secret**

## How to Play

### Concentration Phase

Before starting each level — and at any moment during the game — players can concentrate:

1. Anyone who is ready places the **palm of one hand flat on the table**
2. Once **all players** have placed their hand, everyone removes it and **the game begins**

**Concentrating during the game:** At any moment a player can say **"Stop!"** to pause the game. All players place a palm on the table, refocus, remove their hands... and the game continues! This group concentration before each level is of vital importance to succeed.

### The Golden Rule: Total Silence

**NO communication of any kind is allowed.** This includes:

- ❌ Saying numbers or verbal hints
- ❌ Making intentional gestures or meaningful glances
- ❌ Showing or ordering your cards visibly
- ❌ Making indicative sounds
- ❌ Any kind of agreement or secret signal

### Playing a Level

1. **Any player** may play a card at any time by placing it face-up in the center of the table, forming a pile
2. Cards must come out in **ascending order** — each card played must have a higher value than the previous one
3. **Always play the lowest card in your hand first**: if you have 21, 56, and 93, you must play the 21 first
4. Continue until **all players have run out of cards**

### When a Mistake is Made

If a player plays a card but another player holds a card with a **lower value** in their hand, a mistake has occurred:

1. The player (or players) with lower cards **interrupts the game immediately**
2. The team **loses 1 life** (one life card is set aside)
3. All players **discard** all cards in their hand with a value lower than the card just played
4. The team **concentrates again** and **continues the current level** (it does not restart from scratch!)
5. If no lives remain, **the game ends** and the team loses

### Completing a Level

When all players have run out of cards without making any mistakes:

1. Congratulate each other — you've cleared the level!
2. Check if the completed level card shows a **reward** (in the bottom-right corner)
3. Place the level card in the box
4. Reshuffle all **100 numbered cards** and deal each player **one more card** than the previous level

## Shurikens (Throwing Stars)

Shurikens allow the team to relieve pressure by discarding low cards. To use one:

1. Any player may **propose using a shuriken by raising their hand**
2. If **all** players agree, the shuriken is played: one shuriken card is set aside
3. **Simultaneously**, all players place their card with the **lowest value** face-up next to the central pile
4. The team **concentrates again** and the round continues

**Important:** If any player does not raise their hand, the shuriken is **not used**. You cannot ask or comment anything — it is a collective silent decision.

## Level Rewards

After completing levels **2, 3, 5, 6, 8, and 9**, the team earns a reward:

- 🛡️ **+1 Life** — Take a life from the reserve
- ⭐ **+1 Shuriken** — Take a shuriken from the reserve

The exact reward for each level is shown in the **bottom-right corner of the level card**. The maximum possible is **5 lives** and **3 shurikens**.

## End of the Game

### Victory 🎉

The team **wins** if they complete all the levels in the game without running out of lives.

### Defeat 💀

The team **loses** if they run out of lives at any point during the game.

## Blind Mode (Additional Challenge)

If the team has completed all levels, they can keep playing in **blind mode**:

1. Start again from level 1 with all the lives and shurikens you had left over
2. Now all cards are played **face-down** in the central pile
3. At the end of each level, flip the pile over and verify the order
4. If any player caused a mistake, the team **loses 1 life**
5. All other rules apply unchanged

## The Secret of the Game

The Mind is a game about **synchronization** based on the players' sense of time. The lower a card is, the sooner it should be played. A 5 will likely be played quickly, while an 80 might wait a little longer.

As the game progresses, players increasingly synchronize their sense of time and can better judge when to play each card. What at first seems like mere luck becomes, after a few games, a genuine **skill**. The secret of the game is to develop a shared sense of time: the team must play in harmony... **players must UNITE their minds!**

## Strategies and Tips

- **If you have low cards, play them soon**: A card worth 3 or 7 should probably come out near the beginning
- **If you have high cards, wait**: A card worth 85 is most likely not yours to play until well into the level
- **The last played card gives you information**: If a 45 comes out, all cards from 1 to 45 no longer exist in anyone's hand
- **Propose a shuriken** when too much time has passed without anyone playing — someone likely has a very low card they can't seem to let go of
- **Use the concentration** ("Stop!") to reset the group's rhythm when the team feels disconnected

## History and Trivia

- **Designed by Wolfgang Warsch** (also creator of "The Quacks of Quedlinburg")
- **Published by NSV** in 2018
- **Nominated for the Spiel des Jahres 2018**, one of the most prestigious awards in the board game world
- It has sparked debate in the community: is it truly without communication, or is the game itself a form of communication?
- It has been studied by psychologists for its ability to create **group synchrony** among players
