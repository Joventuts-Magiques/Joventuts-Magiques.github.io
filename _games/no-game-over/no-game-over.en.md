## General Description

No Game Over is a Spanish-made card game inspired by the world of video games. Its tagline says it all: *"The board game that plays like a video game"*. Players gear up with items, fight Enemies, cast Curses on rivals and try to survive while working towards defeating the Final Boss. A fast, chaotic and very fun game where betrayal is part of the rules!

## Game Components

- **Item Deck** — Equipment and Single-Use Item cards
- **Level Deck** — Enemy and Curse cards
- **Final Boss Deck** — final boss cards
- **Hearts** — life tokens for each Player
- **"1st ROUND" tokens** — markers for rounds without reward
- **1 die**

## Game Objective

There are **2 ways to win** No Game Over:

- 🏆 **Defeat the Final Boss**: once you have 6 lives, you can challenge a special Enemy (Final Boss). Beat it and you win the game!
- ☠️ **Kill everyone else**: be the only Player with lives remaining. Last one standing wins.

## Setup

1. Shuffle each of the **3 card decks** separately
2. Deal each Player:
   - **3 Item cards**
   - **3 Level cards**
   - **3 hearts** (lives, visible in their play area)
3. Place the 3 decks face-down in the center of the table, each with its own discard pile next to it
4. Keep the remaining hearts and **"1st ROUND"** tokens accessible on the table
5. Roll the die: **the highest roll goes first**
6. Play proceeds **counter-clockwise**

**Maximum lives:** Each Player can have at most **6 lives**.

## Turn Structure

### 1. Equip (Start of turn)

The active Player places any Equipment they want to use face-up in their play area (visible to everyone). **Non-equipped cards stay in hand**.

**Hand limit: 5 cards maximum.** To reduce your hand you can:
- Cast Curses on other Players
- Use Single-Use Items on yourself (then discard them)
- Discard 3 any cards in exchange for 1 life
- Discard the excess cards until you're down to 5

### 2. Level (Draw a Level card)

The Player draws **1 card from the Level deck** and places it face-up. Depending on the card:

- **Enemy** → an immediate **Combat** begins
- **Curse** → it affects the Player directly

**Important:** Only **1 Level card is revealed per turn**.

### 3. End of turn

If there was a combat, all cards that took part (except Equipment) are discarded.

The active Player may take any actions they want with their cards. They must end with **no more than 5 cards in hand**, then the turn passes to the Player on their **right**.

## Combat

### Your Strength (FUERZA)

**FUERZA = current lives + total strength of all equipped items**

Example: 5 lives + equipment (2+6+2+1+2) = **18 total strength**

To defeat an Enemy, you must **beat their strength**.

### Equipment Slots

You can wear equipment in the following body zones:

| Zone   | Slots |
|--------|-------|
| Head   | 1     |
| Body   | 1     |
| Legs   | 1     |
| Hands  | 2 (or 1 if two-handed) |

### Other Players Interfering

When a Player has more strength than their Enemy, **other Players** can try to harm them by throwing:
- **Single-Use Items**: as many as they want, at any point during combat
- **Curses**: as many as they want, at any point during combat
- **1 secondary Enemy**: its strength is added to the opposing Enemy's strength

**There can be at most 3 Enemies in any single combat:**
- 1 opposing Enemy (drawn from the Level deck)
- 1 secondary Enemy (thrown by another Player)
- 1 allied Enemy (played by the active Player to help themselves)

The active Player can use in their favour: Single-Use Items (as many as they want) + **1 allied Enemy**.

⛔ **A Player CANNOT fight alongside another Player or form alliances to win their combat.**

⛔ **Changing equipment or trading 3 cards for a life is not allowed during combat.**

### Winning Combat

If the Player defeats the Enemy:
- They gain **+1 life**
- They gain the **rewards** from all Enemies defeated (never from the allied Enemy)
- Rewards are Level and/or Item cards, their choice. They cannot look at the cards until they have picked all of them

### Running Away

If the Player cannot win, they may attempt to **run away** by rolling the die. The default escape ability is **2 points** → they escape by rolling **1 or 2**.

Equipment and Curses can modify this:
- Equipment **+1 to escape** → they escape on 1, 2 or 3
- Curse **-2 to escape** → cannot escape with the die (only with an escape card)
- Both at once → escapes only on a 1

**If they escape successfully:** they keep their lives but gain no reward.
**If they fail to escape:** they lose 1 life and gain no reward.

### Special Rule: Easy Mode

If a Player **gets no reward for 2 consecutive turns** (due to running away, Curses, or losing a combat), at the end of that 2nd empty turn they **draw 1 Item or Level card for free**.

The Player picks up a **"1st ROUND"** token when they have a bad turn; that way, if the next turn also yields no reward, they know they can draw their free card.

## Card Types

### Item Deck — Equipment

Increases your strength and abilities. To equip, **place the card face-up on the table visible to all Players**. You can only equip during your turn, **before initiating a Level** and **after finishing a Level** (before ending your turn).

You can swap out equipped items for others and keep the replaced item in your hand.

The card shows:
1. **STRENGTH** it provides
2. **Special Escape Ability** (if any)
3. **Body zone** it occupies (icon)

### Item Deck — Single-Use Items

Can be used for your own benefit (on your turn or in combat) or to harm other Players (at any point during their combat). Must be discarded after use.

Three subtypes:
- **Gain life** → gives hearts to the Player
- **Momentary help** → special temporary effect
- **Extra hit** → additional strength in combat

### Level Deck — Enemy

When an Enemy card is drawn, combat begins immediately. The card shows:
1. **STRENGTH** of the Enemy
2. Whether it **blocks dice escape**
3. **Reward** for defeating it in combat

### Level Deck — Curse

If the Player draws it when initiating Level on their turn, **it affects them directly**. If they collect it as a reward from a defeated Enemy (face-down), they can throw it at any Player **at any moment during the game, whether or not it is their turn**.

Two types:
- **Immediate**: takes effect right away (e.g. -1 life)
- **Non-immediate**: condition-based (e.g. "You cannot use your equipment in your next combat"). Keep the Curse next to your equipment and discard it once it has taken effect

### Final Boss Deck

A Player **can only challenge a Final Boss on their turn**, before initiating a Level, and **only if they have 6 lives**.

To go from 5 to 6 lives: **you must win a combat** (cards that give +1 life cannot be used to reach 6).

In a Final Boss combat:
- Normal combat rules apply
- **Other Players (and Ghosts) cannot intervene at all**: no secondary Enemy, no Curses, no cards of any kind
- If unable to win, the Player may attempt to run away; if they fail, they lose a life

**Defeating the Final Boss = winning the game** 🏆

## When I Lose

### Only 1 life remaining

When a Player has **only 1 life**, **other Players cannot throw a "lose a life" Curse at them**. They can only lose their last life on their own turn: in combat against an Enemy, or if a "lose a life" Curse appears from a Level card.

### No lives left. Game Over... Or NOT?

If a Player runs out of lives, they become a **"Ghost"**. They no longer play normally, but **still take their turn**: they draw 1 Item or Level card of their choice and can use it to harm or help any remaining active Player as they see fit.

Upon dying, the Player must discard all their equipment and hand cards. They immediately draw 1 Level or Item card; if it is a Curse, they may curse someone on the spot.

Cards drawn on their turn that go unused can be kept in hand (maximum 5). **Curses do not affect Ghosts**.

#### Load Game ★

A unique special card found in the Item deck. Whenever it is drawn by any Player or Ghost, **all Ghosts immediately return as Players with 1 life** and the cards they accumulated while in Ghost state.

## Important Clarifications

- **Maximum 6 lives**. From 5 to 6: only by winning a combat.
- **Card trading**: Cards of any kind cannot be given or traded with other players.
- **Swapping equipment**: You can swap equipped items for others and keep the replaced item in your hand.
- **Curse "Spatún"**: If you receive it during your combat, your equipment is disabled for that fight. If received at any other time, you cannot use your equipment in your next combat.
- **Curse "Prince of Bersia"**: Acts as an Enemy that acquires the same strength as the Player it targets at the moment the Curse is received. You can attempt to escape it; if you don't, you lose a life.
- **Momentary help "Robar Thiaf"**: Steal an equipped item from another Player (if it is a card from their hand, you choose at random without seeing what they are).
- **Momentary help "Trampa"**: Lets you equip extra items (2 helmets, 2 two-handed weapons...). This extra equipment cannot be replaced.
- **Momentary help "2nd player"**: Force any Player to help you in your combat. They must add their strength to yours and help with hand cards if needed. If you lose and can't escape, **both of you lose 1 life**. Cannot be used against a Final Boss.

## Discarding Cards

All cards — Item, Level and Final Boss — must be discarded in their respective discard piles, **face-up, stacked in order** so the last discarded card is always visible.

If any of the decks in play runs out of cards, **pick up its discard pile, shuffle it and place it face-down** to replenish that deck.

## Tips and Strategy

- **Track your lives**: Fewer lives means lower base strength. Prioritise winning combats to level up
- **Hold onto Curses**: Curses collected as rewards can be thrown **at any time**, even outside your turn — use them at the perfect moment
- **Don't let anyone reach 6 lives**: Once someone hits 5 lives, everyone else should focus on bringing them down
- **Use Easy Mode**: Don't feel bad about taking the free card — it's there to keep the game balanced
- **Ghosts can decide the outcome**: If you become a Ghost, you can still tip the balance in whoever's favour you choose
